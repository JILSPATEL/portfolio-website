Hello, World!

I am currently pursuing my Master of Technology (MTech) in Computer Science and Engineering at the International Institute of Information Technology, Bangalore (IIIT Bangalore). My academic and engineering journey centers around building rock-solid backend infrastructure, data storage engines, and applied artificial intelligence systems.

Prior to IIIT Bangalore, I earned my BTech in Computer Science and Engineering with specialization in AI & Machine Learning from New LJIET. Over the years, I've honed my craft in low-level systems programming in C and Linux sockets, building database internals in Java from first principles (including SQL parsing and columnar storage with bitmap indexing), and deploying hardened microservices architectures on Kubernetes.

I believe great software is born at the intersection of rigorous algorithmic thinking, clean modular design, and robust automated testing and verification.
